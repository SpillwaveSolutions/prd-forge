+----------------------------------------------------------+
| SC-001 happy path, must                                  |
| Given: A freight operations manager is on section 5 of   |
| their first PRD. Sections 1 and 4 are drafted and        |
| graded. They have 40 minutes before a meeting.           |
+----------------------------------------------------------+
    |
    v
+----------------------------------------------------------+
| When: They say they are ready to do the journeys         |
| section.                                                 |
+----------------------------------------------------------+
    |
    v
+----------------------------------------------------------+
| Sees: They read eight lines on what a user journey is,   |
| why requirements written one at a time miss the steps    |
| between them, and what a stage must name                 |
+----------------------------------------------------------+
    |
    v
+----------------------------------------------------------+
| Sees: They are asked to pick one person and one thing    |
| that person is trying to finish                          |
+----------------------------------------------------------+
    |
    v
+----------------------------------------------------------+
| Does: They say "a dispatcher getting a late load         |
| recovered"                                               |
+----------------------------------------------------------+
    |
    v
+----------------------------------------------------------+
| Sees: They are asked for the big steps, names only, and  |
| give five                                                |
+----------------------------------------------------------+
    |
    v
+----------------------------------------------------------+
| Sees: They are asked, per stage, who acts, what they see |
| and what they decide                                     |
+----------------------------------------------------------+
    |
    v
+----------------------------------------------------------+
| Sees: They are asked which journey is the golden thread, |
| and mark this one                                        |
+----------------------------------------------------------+
    |
    v
+----------------------------------------------------------+
| Sees: They see a drafted journey with five stages, and a |
| grade of 78 with no blocking defects                     |
+----------------------------------------------------------+
    |
    v
+----------------------------------------------------------+
| Sees: They are asked one question - what the dispatcher  |
| decides at stage three - and answer it                   |
+----------------------------------------------------------+
    |
    v
+----------------------------------------------------------+
| Sees: They see the journey re-graded at 88               |
+----------------------------------------------------------+
    |
    v
+----------------------------------------------------------+
| Then: The journeys section is at status draft with a     |
| score of 88 and no blocking defects, one journey is      |
| marked as the golden thread, and every stage names an    |
| actor and a decision.                                    |
+----------------------------------------------------------+

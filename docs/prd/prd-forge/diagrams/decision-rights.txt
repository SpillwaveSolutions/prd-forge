Sponsor decides:
  - TR-001 Approve a PRD for a release (never delegated)

DomainExpert decides:
  - TR-002 Set a section's state to final (never delegated)
  - TR-003 Produce a section's score and findings against
           its rubric (delegable to an agent)
  - TR-004 Decide what the product is for and what is out of
           scope (never delegated)
  - TR-005 State a domain fact, figure, regulation or
           citation as true (never delegated)
  - TR-007 Decide the domain's own terms, and what each one
           means (never delegated)
  - TR-008 Decide the interview on a section is finished
           (never delegated)
  - TR-009 Decide a section may be left at draft and moved
           on from (never delegated)
  - TR-010 Decide the set may be offered for approval (never
           delegated)
  - TR-013 Accept a drafted section as saying what the
           expert meant, or correct it (never delegated)
  - TR-014 Accept or refute a section's score (never
           delegated)

EngineerLead decides:
  - TR-006 Judge whether a set can be built from, and report
           the gaps (delegable to an agent)

DeliveryLead decides:
  - TR-011 Decide a backlog can be mapped from the set
           (delegable to an agent)
  - TR-012 Decide which epics come first (never delegated)

LC-001 a section of the PRD
States: pending, draft, review, final
Starts at pending. Ends at final.

pending --> draft
  When: The expert finishes the section's interview and it
        is written
  Rule: The expert decides the interview is finished
        (TR-008)
  By: DomainExpert

draft --> review
  When: The expert accepts the offer of review
  Rule: A grade of 70 or more, zero blocking defects, and a
        set that checks clean, unless the expert overrides
        the gate and the override is recorded in section 15
  By: DomainExpert

review --> final
  When: The expert sets the section to final
  Rule: The expert's decision under TR-002, after an
        independent grading pass with zero blocking defects,
        unless the expert overrides the gate and the
        override is recorded in section 15. No other
        approval is needed.
  By: DomainExpert

review --> draft
  When: The expert changes the section, because a later
        section shows it is incomplete
  Rule: The expert's decision under TR-002. The section is
        graded again before it returns to review.
  By: DomainExpert

final --> draft
  When: The expert changes the section after it was set
        final
  Rule: The expert's decision under TR-002. If the set was
        approved, the change records a new version that the
        earlier approval does not cover (FR-014). A section
        is never retired, because ids are never reused.
  By: DomainExpert
